God’s answer to overthinking

When your mind will not stop

An overthinking mind is tired, not broken. Scripture does not tell you to think harder. It shows you who to hand the thoughts to, and what to do next.

Thou wilt keep him in perfect peace, whose mind is stayed on thee: because he trusteth in thee.Isaiah 26:3
Be careful for nothing; but in every thing by prayer and supplication with thanksgiving let your requests be made known unto God. And the peace of God, which passeth all understanding, shall keep your hearts and minds through Christ Jesus.Philippians 4:6–7
Take therefore no thought for the morrow: for the morrow shall take thought for the things of itself. Sufficient unto the day is the evil thereof.Matthew 6:34
Nothing you type here is stored.

It is with Him now

You do not have to carry it tonight.

Casting all your care upon him; for he careth for you.1 Peter 5:7

One small step:

  1. Notice the loop

    Name it quietly: “I am overthinking.” Naming a thought gives you a little room to step back from it.

  2. Say it to God

    Pray the whole tangle out loud or on paper. He is not surprised, and He cares about it all.

  3. Test the thought

    Ask whether it is true, whether it is kind, and whether it is yours to carry. Let the rest go.

  4. Swap in a verse

    Choose one line of Scripture and repeat it slowly in place of the argument in your head.

  5. Do one small thing

    Take a walk, answer one message, wash a dish. A small act of obedience loosens the grip.

  6. Rest it again

    “Cast thy burden upon the LORD, and he shall sustain thee.” Hand it over as many times as you need.
