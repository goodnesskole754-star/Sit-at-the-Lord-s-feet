# Sit at the Lord's Feet

A quiet place to draw near. A gentle, single-page devotional website made by **Goodness**.

Come as you are. Rest a while, read His word, and let Him lead the way.

## What's on the site

- **Scripture and reflection**: five short passages with plain-language reflections and a "pray it back" prayer
- **Verse for today**: a verse that changes each day
- **Worship music**: songs and artists for quiet hours, with YouTube and Spotify links
- **Books**: Christian reads, from classics to gentle modern books
- **How you feel**: pick a feeling (anxious, weary, lonely, grateful and more) and get matching Scripture and a short prayer
- **Overthinking**: verses, six small steps, and a "lay it down" box (nothing typed is stored)
- **Community**: a section for encouragement, prayer requests and testimonies

All Scripture is from the King James Version, which is in the public domain.

## Run it locally

No build step or install is needed. Download the files and double-click `index.html` to open it in your browser.

## Put it online

**GitHub Pages**
1. Push these files to a GitHub repository.
2. Go to **Settings, then Pages**.
3. Under "Build and deployment", choose **Deploy from a branch**, pick `main` and `/ (root)`, and save.
4. Your site will appear at `https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/YOUR-REPO/`.

**Netlify**
Drag the project folder onto [app.netlify.com/drop](https://app.netlify.com/drop).

## Good to know

- The **sign up** form saves a profile on the visitor's own device only.
- The **community chat** needs a live database connection to store and show messages. Without one it shows as offline and everything else on the site works as normal.
- Fonts (Cormorant Garamond, Lora, Josefin Sans, Pinyon Script) load from Google Fonts, so an internet connection is needed to see them as designed.
